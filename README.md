# Rose Music Hall — Concert Preview & Jukebox

An interactive music preview web app for upcoming concerts at **Rose Music Hall** (North Village Arts District, Columbia, MO).

## ✨ Features

- **Direct Music Previews**: Listen to 30-second audio clips from bands scheduled to perform.
- **Show Schedule & Dates**: Displays performance dates, door times, supporting lineups, and venue staging (Indoor Club vs. Forrest Rose Park).
- **Genre Filtering**: Filter quickly between:
  - *Indie & Alt*
  - *Country & Americana*
  - *Rock & Grunge*
  - *Funk & Brass*
  - *Folk & Acoustic*
- **Persistent Jukebox Player**:
  - Continuous autoplay mode ("Play All Shows")
  - Scrubbable progress bar
  - Animated live audio visualizer
  - Volume control and mute toggle
- **Event Info & Tickets**: Direct links to official ticketing and full event details.
- **Keyboard Shortcuts**:
  - `Space`: Play / Pause current track
  - `→` (Right Arrow): Skip to next show
  - `←` (Left Arrow): Return to previous show
  - `M`: Mute / Unmute
  - `Esc`: Close detail modal
